Welcome to Sequence! You and an opponent play each other from your phone, tablet, or desktop. Here's how:
  • Player1 taps the "New Game" button. They'll be blue.
  • Then Player2 taps the "Join Game" button. They'll be green.
  • Blue goes first; note the message saying it's blue's turn. The message will change when it's green's turn.
  • The squares where a player can legally play are marked. Player taps a square to place a checker (a second tap removes a checker).
  • Player taps a card in their hand to discard it.
  • Player taps the face down deck to get a new card.
  • When finished, tap the "End Turn" button. Play then transfers to the green player.
  • Just like real life, rule enforcement is up to the players, including those related to playing Jacks. Players decide when the game is done.
